Case 03 · FP&A · Variance analysis

When the bridge does not foot, the story is wrong.

A variance-analysis case built to force reconciliation of segment revenue, foreign-exchange translation, and budget-to-actual drivers. The answer lived across the statements, the segment note, and the management commentary — not in any one of them.

DomainFP&A / variance
DocumentsFS, notes, MD&A
CalibrationThree reviewers
StatusAdopted into the standard set

The problem

Reported revenue moved for three reasons at once: volume and price in the underlying segments, mix, and FX. The management commentary emphasized the most flattering driver. The segment note and the cash-flow statement did not. A correct analysis had to build a bridge that footed to the reported change, isolate translation from transaction effects, and refuse to treat “growth” as a single number.

I wrote the gold-standard bridge, the FX rollforward, and the commentary a finance lead would actually send upstairs: what was operational, what was currency, and what was still unexplained.

Why it is a multi-document case

No single exhibit contained the full answer. Volume had to be inferred from operational commentary, price from the revenue note, and FX from the translation disclosure and average-rate movement. If a model quoted the MD&A without tying it back to the segment table, it failed the core requirement.

That design came out of corporate FP&A work: forecast accuracy is not a narrative skill. It is whether your drivers still explain the actuals two quarters later.

Calibration

Three reviewers scored a first round independently. Disagreements clustered on two points: how much unexplained residual was acceptable, and whether an FX comment without a quantified bridge counted as a pass. We tightened the rubric so residual above a set band was a deduction, and unquantified FX language scored as incomplete rather than correct.

After that calibration, the case went into the team’s standard evaluation set.

Failure modes

Segment totals that did not reconcile to the face of the statements.

Outputs restated segment growth and never checked the corporate total, eliminations, or other.

FX as a residual dump.

Anything not explained by volume was labeled currency, including mix and one-time items disclosed elsewhere.

Budget-to-actual without the original driver pack.

Several analyses compared actuals to last year instead of to the budget the case had provided, which is a different question.
